## Anti-overfit statement

Weights (30/20/15/15/10/10), full-scale constants (4 ATR, 4 z, 2.5 ATR, volume ratio 4, 6 sessions, RSI span 30), the 60 % coverage
floor, the band boundaries and every confidence contribution and cap were fixed in DESIGN_56 before this replay ran and were not
changed after seeing it. No weight search, no threshold selection on returns, no per-ticker thresholds, no model training.

# Stage 5.7 — Market Demand validation (point-in-time, descriptive)

Generated by `python -m demand.replay --db data/stock_agent.db --out scratchpad/s57/replay` on 2026-10-21 (read-only against the main
database). Weights, scales and bands were fixed in `DESIGN_57_MARKET_DEMAND.md` before this replay and were not changed by it. Nothing
here is a predictive claim; outcome diagnostics are descriptive only and were not used to tune anything.

## 1. What could and could not be validated

* **DAILY FALLBACK VALIDATION (real stored history).** The project's only historical bar store is the Stage 3.2 point-in-time daily
  store (82 datasets, 2024-06-03 → 2026-09-28). Every session of every symbol except SPY was scored in `DAILY_MODE` exactly as the live
  fallback does: completed daily bars strictly before the session, components A (completed-day relative volume), D (close location of
  the last completed session), E (1-session and 5-session relative strength vs the benchmark hierarchy) and F (daily follow-through);
  B (VWAP) and C (intraday structure) are NOT_AVAILABLE by design, so coverage sits at the 0.60 floor.
* **INTRADAY VALIDATION (SYNTHETIC).** There is **no intraday history store** in the project and the real Alpaca minute-bar feed is
  not exercised on the development machine, so VWAP acceptance, intraday structure and intraday follow-through were validated on
  deterministic synthetic sessions only: seven constructed scenarios with known structure and 160 simulator sessions (40 symbols
  across semiconductors, mega-cap tech, financials, healthcare, consumer, industrials and energy, scored at 60 / 120 / 180 / 240
  completed bars). These are labelled SYNTHETIC; daily bars are never presented as validating intraday behaviour.

## 5. Volume alone (check 5)

Component A is bounded to [20, 80] by construction (participation × direction); with every other component neutral the total is at most
`0.2·80 + 0.8·50 = 56` (MIXED). In the replay the VERY_WEAK band carries the second-highest relative volume (1.9×), confirming that
volume without price acceptance never scores as demand. The synthetic "volume spike without acceptance" session (3× volume, price below
VWAP all session) scores 10 with A = 20.

## 7. Missing data, lookahead, incomplete sessions, simulator labels (checks 9–12)

* Missing intraday data → `DAILY_MODE` with B and C NOT_AVAILABLE (`INTRADAY_ONLY`), coverage 0.60; missing volume → A NOT_AVAILABLE
  (`NO_VOLUME` / `ZERO_VOLUME`); missing benchmark → E NOT_AVAILABLE (`NO_BENCHMARK`); fewer than two prior sessions → A NOT_AVAILABLE
  (`NO_VOLUME_PROFILE`); below the 0.60 floor → `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, score null (tests E14–E18; 2 such rows in the replay).
* No lookahead: tests L1–L5 append future session bars, future daily bars and future benchmark bars and assert identical results; the
  replay evaluates each session with bars strictly before it and computes outcomes afterwards (L4 shocks the future and shows the
  scores unchanged while the outcomes change); the expected intraday volume curve is a median over prior sessions only.
* Incomplete sessions: every intraday result carries `sources.session.complete = false` until the close and the range component says
  "session so far" (test E23).
* Simulator: every result is `SIMULATED_DATA`, carries the simulator source string and the SIMULATED DATA note (test E24).

## 9. Limitations

No bid/ask microstructure (IEX quotes, synthetic simulator spreads); intraday relative strength uses the session horizon only (no
benchmark minute bars); the real minute-bar path is fake-client-tested, not exercised live here; synthetic sessions are not market data;
daily-mode results never imply real-time confirmation and are labelled DAILY in the UI.
